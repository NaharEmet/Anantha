How can we restructure organisations to be more human and earth scentric?
This is a question that's been with me for the past two decades: why is the world as it is and how can we move it towards a species that we would be proud to have  spread across the stars.
over the last few years. I've come to the understanding that the state of the world we are in is the product of the solutions found for the 2 oldest problems of our species.
the coordination problem and allocation problems.
#### coordination:
how do we get a group of people to make drinks? to make 100 a week a single person can do all of the jobs assuming that we are not counting the upstream and downstream products, glass bottles, sugar, fuel and soo on we could keep looking required inputs and their required inputs and we would eventually go full circle making anything thats not farmed and used by a single person requires the full cooperation of the global economy.
now back to making the drinks we have moved from making 100 to 1000 bottles now  you need people to wash bottles to make the juice to bottle to deliver to talk to clients and soo on as long as its being done by one person its easy just do it if you dont it breaks with 2 people you need to devide roles and trust the other parties this work until about 7 people where you devide the work and have meetings to disscuse what needs to be done when what hasent been done  why and to resolve issues. usually by the 2nd to 5th person there is already a hirarchy formed why is that? usually whoever was there before and thus knows more and whoever has put in the financing to start the project this hirarchy gets built out and expanded apoun we can say that this hirarchy is unjest unfair or wrong but that fails to ask the question why is it there?
the hirarchy is there to filter information and to enable collaberation its almost impossible for a group to agree on what to do which means that people take charge there are people who are more comfortable taking decisions others prefer to do and the problem spaces change now why is there a hirarchy in a 2000 person organisation?
a very good founder can manage 20 people under him above that they will not have time usually by 14 people we start see meetings comitees and leaders out of necessity to communicate information across the organization the founder often becomes the bridge across departments now as we are trying to setup a non hirarchichal organisation we will create groups the aspiration is that the people in these groups will take the decisions and then execute this works until around 200 people at this point groups start to create sub groups this is a hierarchy but instead of an individual hierarchy its a group hierarchy the hope is that these groups will be democratic this is rarly the case as people do not like to be liable and to take decisions these groups will usually have one person taking the decisions and the others voting with them. i have rarely seen a group that was democratic and not just agreeing to one person the other issues with groups is that if there are players there for a while and they control who comes in in any form they'll form a group that's subservient to them. and thus our group based non hierarchical organisation has collapsed back into a standard hierarchical system 
The best solution we have found for these problems is hierarchy for coordination and money and markets for resource allocation. In Auroville , a project that tried to go beyond them, I've watched the evolution and studied the origins of these 2 systems, how they came in as easy answers to difficult questions. From what I've seen with the tools of the old world, the world we are in now is the best construct.
 With the advent of AI I see an opportunity to use it to allocate resources and move information across organizations in a way that we could have only dreamed of 5 years ago. I've been building experiments and I want to work with other people to build and deploy new solutions to the 2 fundamental problems faced by humanity. to allow people to have a better relationship with each other and with our planet
